a) 
Agile vs non Agile
Pros
- Able to rapidly change direction. Faster turn around.
- Constantly being built by feedback. Ever evolving to reach a better state.
Cons
- More pushes mean more times for somehting to break. less predictable.
- Normally leads to poorer documentation due to fast development.

b)

Scrum Master:
- In charge of how development occurs
- Plans Sprints 
- Run Meetins

Project Owner
- External Representation
- Deals with the goals
- Handles Releases

c)

Sprint Planning Meeting:
- Determine goals in the sprint
- Move stories in sprint from backlog
- Determine story points and assign stories to teams
- Start sprint

Daily Scrum/Standup Meeting:
- Discuss work completed
- Plan for the day and add any blockers

Sprint Retrospective Meeting:
- Discuss work complete 
- Move incomplete work to backlog
- Close sprint

d)

Epic:
- Largest Tier, Made up of stories and tasks
- Can last multiple Sprints


Story:
- Singular requirment
- User/stakeholder created Normally
- Tried to be accomplished in one sprint

Bug:
- An issue that blocks a Story and or Epic
- Stops development of one branch/task
- Given from external and or internal testing

Task:
- Normally subparts of Stories.
- Needed action item to complete a requirment
- complete multiple in a sprint

a) 
Agile vs non Agile
Pros
- Able to rapidly change direction. Faster turn around.
- Constantly being built by feedback. Ever evolving to reach a better state.
Cons
- More pushes mean more times for somehting to break. less predictable.
- Normally leads to poorer documentation due to fast development.

Scrum Master:
- In charge of how development occurs
- Plans Sprints 
- Run Meetins

Project Owner
- External Representation
- Deals with the goals
- Handles Releases
